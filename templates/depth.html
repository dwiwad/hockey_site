Understanding Depth Metrics

What You're Seeing

Each bar shows how evenly a team distributed shots, scoring chances, and ice time across their lineup. Higher depth means more players contributed, rather than relying on a few stars.

The Four Components

  • Shot Depth: How evenly distributed shots on goal were
  • CF Depth: How evenly distributed shot attempts were (Corsi For)
  • xG Depth: How evenly distributed expected goals were
  • TOI Depth: How evenly ice time was distributed

Note: These metrics show depth in this specific game. For season trends and historical analysis, I use a standardized Total Depth Index (TDI) calculated using structural equation modeling factor scores. Learn more about the methodology →
